# Profile

The Profilo page (`/profile`) shows your own data and lets you change your name.

## Your data

The page shows your email and your role ("Admin" or "Giocatore"). You cannot change them here.

## Change your name

1. Edit "Nome" and "Cognome", 1 to 50 characters each.
2. Press "Salva".

The page confirms with "Profilo aggiornato". The new name shows at once in the header and in the Rosa page. When a name is empty or too long, the page shows the message of the app and keeps your old name.

A player can change only their own name. An admin can also [rename any player](invite-links.md#rename-a-player) from Rosa.

The page has no password change. To set a new password, ask an admin for an [invite link](invite-links.md#set-a-new-password).
